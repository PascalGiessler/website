# Product

## Product Purpose
Portfolio website for Dr. Pascal Giessler — Principal AI & AI Lead at Haufe Akademie, Mentor and Strategic Advisor, with a PhD from KIT. Serves as the primary professional identity hub connecting LinkedIn presence, Substack writing (Stack und Kalkül, The Principal Stack), and direct professional outreach.

## Users
- **Primary**: AI leaders, CTOs and engineering leaders evaluating Pascal for AI leadership, advisory or mentoring; hiring decision-makers for Head-of-AI and leadership roles
- **Secondary**: Mid-level engineers and researchers following technical content on AI-native engineering, cloud architecture, and generative AI strategy
- **Geography**: DACH region (Freiburg-centered) with broader German enterprise tech reach

## Brand
- **Identity**: Dr. Pascal Giessler — Principal AI & AI Lead at Haufe Akademie, responsible for organisational AI transformation, working closely with the CTO and supporting strategic initiatives. Mentor and Strategic Advisor. Confirmed background: PhD (KIT), MIT Technology & Innovation, Azure Solutions Architect Expert, earlier company founder. Website-specific supporting roles confirmed on 2026-10-25: Ex-CTO and Stealth CEO. Further projects are not yet public; disclose no details
- **Tone**: Clear and concrete, technically precise, grounded in confirmed responsibility. Use the Humanizer skill for public prose. No invented achievements or grand claims
- **Color anchor**: Deep warm black with amber-gold accents (mirrors LinkedIn header: gold neural network on black)
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS landing pages, purple-gradient AI startup aesthetic, white + teal "innovation" templates, hero-metric dashboards, identical card grids

## Strategic Principles
1. AI leadership and organisational transformation lead the story, supported by technical depth and readable, accessible presentation
2. Use the shared PROFILE and ROLE in src/data/entity.ts; qualifications support the current responsibility rather than replace it
3. Dark by design — the brand identity IS dark
4. GitHub Pages static hosting (Astro static output, no server-side)
5. Copy: no em dashes, every word earns its place
