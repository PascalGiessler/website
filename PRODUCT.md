# Product

## Product Purpose
Portfolio website for Dr. Pascal Giessler — Principal AI & Cloud Architect, PhD researcher (KIT), MIT-trained, and technical thought leader. Serves as the primary professional identity hub connecting LinkedIn presence, Substack writing (The Engineer's Library, Path to Scale), and direct professional outreach.

## Users
- **Primary**: Senior engineers, software architects, technical founders, CTO-level decision-makers evaluating Pascal as an advisor, collaborator, or thought leader
- **Secondary**: Mid-level engineers and researchers following technical content on AI-native engineering, cloud architecture, and generative AI strategy
- **Geography**: DACH region (Freiburg-centered) with broader German enterprise tech reach

## Brand
- **Identity**: Dr. Pascal Giessler — AI Principal with PhD (KIT Karlsruhe), MIT Technology & Innovation, 9-year CTO tenure at a DACH engineering services consultancy, Azure Certified, currently leading strategic AI at Haufe Akademie
- **Tone**: Authoritative, mentor-like, authentic — technical credibility first, strategic implications second
- **Color anchor**: Deep warm black with amber-gold accents (mirrors LinkedIn header: gold neural network on black)
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS landing pages, purple-gradient AI startup aesthetic, white + teal "innovation" templates, hero-metric dashboards, identical card grids

## Strategic Principles
1. Technical credibility is primary — every design choice signals depth, not accessibility
2. Credentials are load-bearing: PhD, MIT, CTO, Azure Expert certifications are identity signals
3. Dark by design — the brand identity IS dark
4. GitHub Pages static hosting (Astro static output, no server-side)
5. Copy: no em dashes, every word earns its place
